Each fold is the opposite type on this side. Fully opened, the inside reads left to right: P5 → P6 → P7 → P8.

Duplex (two-sided) printing

  • Turn the sheet on its short edge (left ↔ right), keeping the top edge at the top.
  • Back-to-back pairs: P4 ↔ P5, P3 ↔ P6, P2 ↔ P7, P1 ↔ P8.
  • Fold lines on both sides must line up exactly; panels are equal width (standard for accordion folds).
  • Folding direction: P1|P2 mountain, P2|P3 valley, P3|P4 mountain (seen from the outside).

P5 + P6 — revealed when the cover is opened

P1 — BACK COVER (face down)

Reading sequence

  • Closed: front cover P4 on top, back cover P1 underneath.
  • Paging through like a booklet: P4 → P5|P6 → P3|P2 → P7|P8 → P1.
  • Fully opened, inside up: all four service panels P5 → P8 in one row.
  • Fully opened, outside up: P1 back cover · P2 overview · P3 introduction · P4 front cover.
To confirm with the printing agency before the print PDF is made:
1. Exact K4 sheet size (width × height) and whether panels stay equal width.
2. Paper stock and weight (e.g. glossy art paper, gsm) and whether creasing is used.
3. Bleed (3 mm assumed) and whether crop / fold marks are required.
4. Duplex turn: short edge (assumed here) or long edge.
5. Colour: whether they accept RGB PDF and convert to CMYK, or require a CMYK/PDF-X file.
